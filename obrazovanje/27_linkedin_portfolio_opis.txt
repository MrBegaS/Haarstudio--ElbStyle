Premium Hair Salon Landing Page – Haarstudio ElbStyle

I designed and built a modern, responsive landing page for a fictional premium hair salon in Hamburg. The project focused on luxury brand presentation, mobile-first layout, clear service communication, and conversion-oriented design.

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the site includes:
- premium aesthetic and typography
- responsive layout for mobile and desktop
- service and pricing sections
- gallery interaction
- testimonials and social proof
- contact form and CTA areas
- SEO-friendly structure and metadata

This project helped me practice real front-end work beyond basic coding: planning, layout decisions, UX flow, client-focused content, and deployment-ready presentation.

CV / PORTFOLIO TEKST

I created a premium, responsive landing page for a fictional luxury hair salon in Hamburg, using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The goal was to design a modern online presence that reflects a high-end salon brand and communicates trust, quality, and professionalism.

The project included:
- brand-oriented homepage design
- mobile-friendly responsive layout
- service and pricing sections
- gallery with visual interaction
- testimonials and social proof
- clear call-to-action and contact form
- SEO-friendly structure and metadata

This project helped me strengthen my front-end skills in design, layout structure, user experience, and business-focused web development. It also showed my ability to build a complete project from concept to deployment-ready presentation.

I am especially interested in creating modern, clean, and user-focused websites that combine visual quality with practical business goals.
